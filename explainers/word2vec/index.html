Every word is a point in a 300-dimensional space, learned from ~100 billion words of Google News. This page runs entirely in your browser on the 100,000 most frequent words (8-bit compressed, 30 MB). Words used in similar contexts end up close together — and directions in the space carry meaning.

Each row is one word: 300 numbers, blue = negative, red = positive (unit-length vectors).
